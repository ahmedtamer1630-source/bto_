POLICIES

HOW TO ORDER

Pick your jacket and size, press Order now, then fill in your name, phone number and full delivery address. Press Send order and an email with all your details opens, ready to send to beyondtheordinary000@gmail.com.

We reply within 24 hours to confirm availability, the total and the payment method. Your order is only confirmed once you receive our reply.

SHIPPING
  • Orders are prepared within 1–3 business days after confirmation.
  • Delivery inside Egypt takes 2–5 business days depending on your city.
  • You get a tracking or courier contact number once your order ships.
  • Please check the parcel when it arrives and tell us right away if anything is wrong.
RETURNS & EXCHANGES

14 days, no reason needed. You can return or exchange your jacket within 14 days of receiving it, in line with Egyptian Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018.

  • The jacket must be unworn, unwashed, with its tags and in its original packaging.
  • We cover the return shipping for returns and exchanges.
  • Exchanges (another size or colour) are subject to availability. If the item you want is out of stock, you get a refund.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the item.

Defective or wrong item. If your jacket arrives damaged, defective or not as described, tell us within 30 days of delivery. We replace it or refund you in full, and we pay for shipping.

To start a return: email us with your name, order details and a short note (add photos if the item is defective). We reply with the next steps.

SIZE GUIDE

The BTO wool jacket has a boxy fit with dropped shoulders, and the sizes run relaxed. Pick your usual size for the intended look, or size down if you prefer a closer fit. Not sure? Email us your height and weight and we'll recommend a size.

CARE
  • Dry clean recommended.
  • Spot clean gently with a damp cloth.
  • Do not tumble dry or iron the embroidery directly.
  • Store on a wide hanger.
PRIVACY

We only use the details you send us (name, phone, address) to prepare and deliver your order and to answer your questions. We never sell or share them, except with the courier that delivers your order.
