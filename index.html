SHIPPING & RETURNS

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

  • We deliver across Egypt.
  • Orders are prepared within 1–2 working days.
  • Delivery takes 1–3 working days in Cairo & Giza, and 3–5 working days in other governorates.
  • Free shipping on orders over LE 3,000. Below that, we confirm the shipping fee with you on WhatsApp before anything ships.
  • We send you the courier details as soon as your order leaves us.

PAYMENT

  • Cash on delivery.
  • Check the package with the courier when it arrives, before you pay.

EXCHANGES

  • You have 14 days from the day you receive your order.
  • Your first size exchange is free.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags and original packaging.
  • Exchanges depend on stock. If your size is gone, we refund you.

RETURNS & REFUNDS

  • Return within 14 days of receiving your order, in the condition above.
  • We refund the item price within 7 working days after the item reaches us. Return shipping is paid by the customer.
  • Defective or wrong item? Tell us and we cover everything: a replacement or a full refund, shipping included.

CANCELLATIONS

  • Cancel anytime before your order ships, free of charge.

HOW TO START

Message us on WhatsApp with your name, phone number and order details (and a photo if there is an issue). We reply within one working day.
